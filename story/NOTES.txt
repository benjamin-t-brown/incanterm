Johnathan - A somewhat hapless boy who is trying to ask a date, Vivi, to prom.
Otis - Johnathan's friend, Vivi's younger brother.  Surly but encouraging.
Vivi - Crush of Johnathan's, quiet, athletic, reserved.
Dedrick - Man overseeing the Revirtualis in the school, overworked on a deadline, somewhat desperate, catalyst
Jeremiah - The "bug".  He is sabotaging the Revirtualis for Regem Ludos reasons, setting up surveillance and contrived happenstance meant for Ada, but Johnathan keeps tripping into it.  He is a higher up scientist at the same place Dedrick works

Settings
- School (Erran Valley)
- Revirtualis (various)


Johnathan is bribed into entering a sketchy Revirtualis by the scientist Dedrick because he needs to calibrate the teleporter.  He tells Jonathan to go in there and find the "Bug" in the system. Johnathan jumps into a wacky world solving puzzles, talking to people and trying to do the best job he can.  He takes a while though and has some struggles. Dedrick sends in other students after him, as his deadline is coming up.  Johnathan and the other students get stuck as he's pursuing the Bug, as he is too focused on his task, and it gets them into trouble.  He finally concedes that they should try to get out, and they work together to do that.  On the way out, the "Bug" appears, and Johnathan eventually defeats it, and he and the others get out of the Revirtualis.  When they get out, the bug is solved and Dedrick says its ready to go.  Jeremiah appears and relieves him of his duty, and the students split the pay.
