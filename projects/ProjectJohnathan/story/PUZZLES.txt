- Science closet

  - Johnathan and Vivi get teleported into the Science Closet. It is locked from the outside by a thin metal door. Inside is old machines, crates, and storage from years past. A massive focus magnet sits on a cart. Sent at the door at high speed, it knocks the door down. The magnet needs two batteries before it will run.

  - Goals
    - Build rapport with Vivi and Johnathan
    - Solve a more difficult puzzle
    - Build tension with setting
    - More item-based puzzle

  - Steps
    - Find the old textbook on the shelf. A forgetful teacher wrote the terminal password on the last page, but that page is ripped, so the password is incomplete.
    - On the old terminal, which is still running and connected to a database of school stuff, look up the other books assigned to that teacher.
    - Use the other machine to fetch those books from the Book Vault.
    - Read the complete password on the last page of a fetched book.
    - Type the password into the terminal. That puts it in maintenance mode and unlocks its internals.
    - Take battery 1 out of the terminal.
    - Find battery 2 elsewhere in the room.
    - Install both batteries in the focus magnet, send the cart into the door, and knock it down.
